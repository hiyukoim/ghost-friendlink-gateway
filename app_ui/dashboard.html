Privacy & Housekeeping

  • Every access log stores IP, user agent, and referer domain for leak detection. Adjust ACCESS_LOG_RETENTION_DAYS or run the cleanup API if you need shorter retention.
  • Let your members know that accesses are tracked; update your privacy policy accordingly.
  • Use HTTPS + a reverse proxy/WAF in production, and rotate the Basic/ADMIN keys periodically.
